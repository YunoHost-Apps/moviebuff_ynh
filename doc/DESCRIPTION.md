MovieBuff is a federated social network for films and TV series, an alternative to Letterboxd built on ActivityPub.

### Features

- Log what you watch, episode by episode for series, with a grade, a review and notes
- Watchlist, watched shelves, lists shared with other members, release calendar
- Follow people on MovieBuff, Mastodon, NeoDB and the rest of the Fediverse, reply, like and boost
- Import your history from Letterboxd, MyAnimeList or Jellyfin, and connect your own Jellyfin server
- Four post visibilities, follower approval, blocks, reports and domain moderation
- Installable on a phone (PWA), with push notifications
- English and French interface
